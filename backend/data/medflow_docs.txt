# MedFlow Clinic Platform Documentation

## Booking Appointments
Patients can book appointments easily online without needing to create an account or log in. 
To book an appointment, navigate to the 'Book Appointment' section on the homepage, select a specialist from the dropdown menu, choose a preferred date and time, and submit your personal details.
Appointments are marked as 'Pending' immediately after booking.

## Approval Workflow
Once an appointment is requested, it enters the Doctor Dashboard.
The specific doctor assigned to the appointment will review the request and can either 'Approve' or 'Reject' it.
If the doctor clicks 'Approve', an automated webhook is triggered to the n8n automation engine.
The n8n automation engine integrates with Twilio to instantly send an SMS confirmation to the patient's mobile phone number.

## Available Specialties
MedFlow Clinic currently offers specialists in the following domains:
1. Cardiology (Advanced heart care and cardiovascular surgery)
2. Neurology (Expert diagnosis and treatment of brain and nervous system disorders)
3. Pediatrics (Compassionate care for infants, children, and adolescents)
4. General Surgery (Comprehensive surgical interventions)
5. Lab Diagnostics (State-of-the-art testing for rapid, accurate medical results)

## Doctor Portal & Dashboard
The Doctor Portal is highly secure and requires a Firebase-authenticated login.
Doctors must register with their Full Name, Degree (e.g., MBBS, MD), and Specialty.
Inside the dashboard, doctors can view their personalized schedule. They will only see appointments assigned to their specific doctor ID.
The dashboard features an AI-powered "Synthesize Clinical Data" engine.

## GraphRAG Clinical Synthesis Engine
The MedFlow system uses an advanced Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG) architecture powered by Neo4j.
Instead of standard vector embeddings, patient medical records (Conditions, Medications, Allergies) are mapped as interconnected nodes in a graph database.
When a doctor requests a synthesis, AI agents execute Cypher queries to traverse the graph.
A specialized Safety Agent analyzes the graph for `CONFLICTS_WITH` relationships to instantly flag dangerous drug interactions (e.g., if a patient takes Ibuprofen while diagnosed with Hypertension).

## General Policy
If patients experience a medical emergency, they should call emergency services immediately. MedFlow AI is a scheduling and clinical synthesis tool, not an emergency response system.
